# Korean Copy Standard

Write for an anxious Korean reader who needs orientation, not for another lawyer and not for a search crawler.

## Drafting rules

- Start with the reader's recognizable situation, then introduce the legal term.
- Keep one main claim per sentence. Split stacked conditions and three-or-more noun chains.
- Prefer active, concrete verbs: `보존합니다`, `정리합니다`, `확인합니다`, `제출합니다`, `다툽니다`.
- Name the object of vague advice. Replace `초기 대응이 중요합니다` with what to preserve, avoid, or prepare.
- Retain necessary legal terms, then explain their practical meaning once.
- Use `~합니다` for explanatory copy. Vary openings and sentence lengths within that register.
- Separate accused/person-under-investigation and victim guidance when their actions conflict.

## Repetition scan

Review the complete leaf, not isolated arrays. Flag:

- the same opening or ending in three or more nearby items;
- repeated `핵심`, `중요`, `필요`, `전문적인 조력`, `초기 대응` without new information;
- mechanical triples and identical two-bullet rhythms;
- `사안에 따라 달라집니다` used as a substitute for an answer;
- profile credentials repeated in the lead, attorney section, FAQ, meta description, and CTA;
- every attorney-role item beginning with `정리합니다` or ending with `검토합니다`.

Vary substance first, syntax second. Do not add decorative synonyms to disguise duplicate meaning.

## Unnatural or risky patterns

Rewrite:

- translation-like phrases such as `~에 대해`, `~을 통해`, or passive chains when a direct verb works;
- consultancy language such as `솔루션`, `로드맵`, `리스크 관리`, `전략적 접근` when plain legal work is clearer;
- textbook definitions that do not help self-classification;
- emotional pressure, fear-heavy penalty lists, and commands to hire counsel;
- claims that a single statement or first interview determines the result;
- `A가 아니라 B` contrast formulas used as empty emphasis;
- keyword strings disguised as sentences.

## Review sequence

1. **Humanizer**: inspect AI repetition, translation patterns, vague abstractions, legal-domain vocabulary, advertising risk, and sentence complexity.
2. **Grammar checker**: correct spelling, spacing, particles, endings, and punctuation without changing legal meaning.
3. **Style guide**: standardize voice, terminology, number/date forms, labels, and list endings across all leaves.
4. **Fact-preservation pass**: compare names, credentials, dates, statutes, procedure, party position, negation, and degree of certainty with the sourced draft.

Humanization must never introduce a new legal proposition or strengthen `may` into `will`.

## Acceptance sample

Weak:

`초기 대응이 매우 중요하며 전문적인 법률 조력을 통해 효과적인 전략을 수립할 수 있습니다.`

Better shape:

`조사 일정을 통보받았다면 출석 전에 당시 대화와 이동 경로를 시간순으로 정리하고, 남아 있는 메시지와 결제 기록을 보존해야 합니다.`

The second sentence is not universally reusable. Replace its evidence and action for each case type.
