# Quality Gates

Mark each gate `pass`, `fail`, or `approval needed`. Do not call a page complete while a `fail` remains.

## Source and compliance

- [ ] Locked IA labels, slugs, and route hierarchy are preserved.
- [ ] Changeable legal facts are checked against current official sources.
- [ ] Every published profile claim is verified and approved; conflicts are surfaced.
- [ ] No experience claim implies relationships, influence, or guaranteed results.
- [ ] Specialty wording matches the exact registered field and does not create certification confusion.
- [ ] No result guarantee, success statistic, unsupported superlative, competitor comparison, free/discount claim, or fear copy appears.
- [ ] Public phone, office name, attorney name, and advertising-responsibility labels follow the currently approved source; conflicts are not guessed away.

## Visitor value

- [ ] The page answers one distinct primary intent.
- [ ] A visitor can classify the matter from concrete situations.
- [ ] Evidence, procedure, immediate action, and attorney work are specific to this leaf.
- [ ] Accused and victim guidance is separated when necessary.
- [ ] The page adds unique value beyond sibling leaves and is not a doorway.
- [ ] FAQ answers add information rather than repeat sales claims.
- [ ] The footer CTA names a page-specific situation or decision point and gives one direct next action.
- [ ] Footer CTA urgency matches the matter and does not become a repeated summary, keyword block, or fear cue.

## Korean quality

- [ ] No generic advice remains without a named action or object.
- [ ] Full-page repetition, identical sentence frames, translation patterns, and keyword strings were reviewed.
- [ ] Humanizer, grammar, and style passes ran in order.
- [ ] Final review preserved facts, negation, party position, and uncertainty.

## Search and implementation

- [ ] Title, H1, description, visible lead, and body align.
- [ ] Title and description are unique among service leaves.
- [ ] One visible H1 exists; heading hierarchy remains coherent.
- [ ] Canonical/indexability follow the project convention.
- [ ] Internal links are relevant and use descriptive anchors.
- [ ] Structured data matches visible content and validates when changed.
- [ ] Touched code passes relevant lint/type checks.
- [ ] Changed UI is inspected at desktop and mobile widths.

## Final comparison

Compare the target leaf with at least two implemented siblings. If three pages could exchange their titles without the body feeling wrong, the copy is still too generic.
